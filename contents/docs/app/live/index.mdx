## Live Race Dashboard

The **Live** tab is a real-time dashboard built for driving, not just glancing at. It updates
continuously while your N-Link is streaming, and switches layout automatically between portrait
and landscape.

<Note type="note" title="Live data only">
Like the [Home dashboard](/docs/app/dashboard), every reading needs an active
[connection](/docs/app/pairing) to your N-Link with the vehicle running. Fields show `--` instead
of a stale or zero value when there's no live data.
</Note>

## Portrait: Command Deck

Turn the phone upright for a scrollable "command deck" layout:

- **Main card** — boost pressure and drive mode on the left, a large gear readout in the center,
  RPM and speed on the right.
- **Pedals** — horizontal throttle (THR) and brake (BRK) bars with live percentages.
- **Engine tiles** — coolant temp, oil temp, intake temp, and oil pressure in a 2×2 grid.
- **Tire pressure** — a plain FL / FR / RL / RR grid, in psi.
- **Footer** — fuel level and 12V battery voltage.

On tablets, the engine tile grid and tire pressure card sit side by side instead of stacking.

## Landscape: full-screen dashboard

Rotate to landscape and the Live tab goes full-screen (status bar hidden) for an immersive
dashboard:

- **Top strip** — a segmented shift-light bar that fills up with RPM, a drive-mode badge, and a
  LIVE/OFFLINE status pill.
- **Center** — a large gear digit with a left/right steering-angle bar underneath.
- **Side columns** — swipeable card stacks. The left column has boost/coolant/oil-temp/oil-pressure
  tiles, a G-force card (lateral, longitudinal, and peak G with a live plot), and a second tile
  page with fuel, odometer, ambient temperature, and voltage. The right column has a speed/RPM
  card with throttle and brake bars, and a tire-pressure grid.
- **Bottom strip** — telltale lamps for turn signals, ABS, ESC, TPMS, oil pressure, coolant/oil
  temperature, battery, and fuel, each lighting up in its warning color when active.

<Note type="warning" title="Some telltales are always dark">
Turn-signal and ABS lamps exist in the layout but have no CAN decode on any supported vehicle
yet, so they never light up. This isn't a bug — it's reserved for future vehicle support.
</Note>

## Drive mode

The mode box/badge shows your vehicle's active drive mode: **Normal**, **Eco**, **Sport**, **N**
(shown with the N logo), or **Custom**.

## G-force

The G-force card plots your current lateral and longitudinal acceleration as a dot inside a
2-ring gauge (rings at 1G and 2G), alongside live LAT/LON readings and the peak combined G
recorded since you opened the tab.
