## What's New Walkthrough

KDMLink occasionally shows a short, full-screen walkthrough when you open the app — a handful of
swipeable pages, each with a short looping demo clip, introducing a feature from a recent update.

<Note type="note" title="Not every update shows one">
This only appears for updates that ship something worth walking through. Routine updates and bug
fixes don't trigger it, so you won't see it every time you update the app.
</Note>

## When it appears

The walkthrough shows once, automatically, the first time you reach the main app after installing
KDMLink or after updating to a version with new walkthrough content — you don't need to do
anything to trigger it. Once you've seen a given walkthrough, KDMLink remembers that and won't
show it again unless a later update ships new content.

## Getting through it

- **Swipe** left/right, or tap **Next**, to move between pages. Progress dots at the bottom show
  where you are.
- **Skip** (top right) closes the walkthrough immediately from any page except the last.
- On the last page, **Skip** is replaced by **Done**, since that's how you'd normally finish it.
- On Android, the system back button also closes it.
