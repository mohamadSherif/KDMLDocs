## Sessions

The **Sessions** tab records a drive's telemetry for later review, and tracks your all-time
personal bests across every recorded session.

## Recording a session

<Step>
<StepItem title="Connect and select a vehicle">

Recording needs an active N-Link [connection](/docs/app/pairing) and a vehicle selected in
Settings. If either is missing, the app tells you what to fix before it will start.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Start">

Tap **Start** on the timer card at the top of Sessions. The card expands into a full active-session
screen with a running timer and a live speed gauge.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Stop and name it">

Tap **Stop**, then give the session a name in the dialog that appears. The session is saved to
your Past Sessions list.

</StepItem>
</Step>

  <Card subtitle="Stopping" title="Max Braking G" description="Highest braking G-force." />

  <Card subtitle="Output" title="Peak Torque" description="Highest calculated torque." />

Each record shows the date it was set. Records update automatically as new sessions come in —
there's nothing to reset or configure.

## Past Sessions

Every completed session appears as a card with its name, date, duration, top speed, and drive
mode. Tap a card to open the full session detail, or swipe it away to delete (with a confirmation
prompt first).

### Session detail

Opening a session shows its full peak stats — top speed, peak boost, peak RPM, max G, peak power,
and peak torque — plus time-series charts for turbo boost, RPM, speed, and oil temperature over
the course of the drive.

## Sharing a session

Sessions can be shared two ways from the session detail screen: as plain text summarizing the
drive, or as a shareable card image with the session's stats laid out for posting. Both use your
device's native share sheet.
