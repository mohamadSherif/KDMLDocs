## Octane Learning

After every refuel, your ECU restarts its knock-adaptation cycle and temporarily caps peak boost
until it verifies the new fuel's quality. The **Octane Learning** screen shows where your car is
in that cycle. Open it from the **Octane Learning** widget on [Home](/docs/app/dashboard), or from
the Fuel Quality card on the Turbo widget's detail screen.

<Note type="note" title="Availability depends on your vehicle">
Octane learning only appears for vehicles that support it — see the widget list on
[Home Dashboard](/docs/app/dashboard).
</Note>

## Why it isn't always instant

Your car doesn't broadcast the exact moment learning finishes. On vehicles that broadcast ECU
progress flags, KDMLink reads those directly, but the final "Learned" confirmation can still lag
a drive or two behind. On vehicles that don't broadcast progress at all, KDMLink tracks the cycle
from the refuel itself and only confirms completion from the boost behavior of a full-throttle
pull.

## Confirming with a pull

On vehicles without a broadcast status flag, the screen shows a **Verify with a Pull** section:
on a closed course, one full-throttle pull through the vehicle's verification RPM band (shown on
screen) in 3rd or 4th gear. KDMLink reads the boost behavior through that pull to confirm learned
vs. still-clamped instantly, and shows the last pull's verdict.

<Note type="warning" title="Closed-course only">
Full-throttle verification pulls should only be done somewhere it's legal and safe to do so, like
a track or closed course.
</Note>
