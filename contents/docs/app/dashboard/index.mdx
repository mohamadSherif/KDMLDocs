## Home Dashboard

The **Home** tab is a customizable grid of live widget cards, each showing a different set of
vehicle data decoded from the CAN bus your **N-Link** is connected to. Add, remove, and reorder
widgets to build the dashboard that matters for your car.

<Note type="note" title="Live data only">
Every widget needs an active [connection](/docs/app/pairing) to your N-Link with the vehicle
running. If a widget has no data yet — for example the car isn't on, or that signal hasn't
arrived — it shows an empty state instead of a stale or zero reading.
</Note>

Which widgets are available depends on your selected vehicle — for example, octane learning only
appears for vehicles that support it.

The default layout on first launch pairs **Tire Pressure** and **Fuel** on top, a full-width
**Car Health** card in the middle, then **Battery** and **Turbo** beneath, followed by **Power &
Torque** and **Octane**.

## Tap a card for details

Tapping any widget opens a full detail screen for that signal, including a short history chart
where available (for example, Car Health shows recent coolant, oil, and ambient temperature
trends, not just the current reading).

## Add, remove, and reorder widgets

<Step>
<StepItem title="Open Add Widget">

On the Home tab, open the widget gallery to see every widget not currently on your dashboard,
shown as live preview tiles.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Add a widget">

Tap a widget in the gallery to add it to the bottom of your Home dashboard. Widgets already on
Home appear dimmed in the gallery since they're already active.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Reorder or remove">

From the Home screen, drag a widget to reorder it, or remove one you don't need. Your layout is
saved on the device and persists across app restarts.

</StepItem>
</Step>
