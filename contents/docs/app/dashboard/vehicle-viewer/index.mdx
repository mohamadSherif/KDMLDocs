## 3D Vehicle Viewer

At the top of the **Home** tab, KDMLink renders your car as an interactive 3D model instead of a
static photo — for the vehicles it has a model for. Drag left or right to spin it around, or press
and hold it to change its paint.

<Note type="note" title="Model availability depends on your vehicle">
The 3D model currently covers the Hyundai Veloster N, Elantra N, and Kona N. Any other selected
vehicle shows the standard static vehicle image instead, in the same spot.
</Note>

## Rotating the car

The model is locked to a side-profile camera angle — it doesn't tilt up or down — but you can drag
horizontally to orbit it around and view any angle. Vertical drags pass through to the Home tab's
scroll instead of moving the car.

## Repainting your car

Press and hold the car for about a second to open the **Paint** sheet.

<Step>
<StepItem title="Hold the car">

Press and hold anywhere on the 3D model. A short haptic tap confirms the paint sheet is opening.
The Home tab stays visible and undimmed behind the sheet so you can see your changes as you make
them.

</StepItem>
<StepItem title="Pick a color">

Tap one of the five stock swatches (Phantom Black, Ultimate Red, Intense Blue, Atlas White,
Performance Blue) for a one-tap repaint, or use the color wheel below it for any custom shade —
drag around the wheel for hue and saturation, and use the bar underneath for brightness. The
model repaints live as you drag.

</StepItem>
<StepItem title="Done">

Tap **Done** to close the sheet. Your choice is saved for this vehicle and reloads the next time
you open the app.

</StepItem>
</Step>

<Note type="note" title="Saved per vehicle">
Paint color is remembered separately for each vehicle profile. It also carries over to other
places KDMLink renders your car in 3D, such as shared [session](/docs/app/sessions) card images.
</Note>

## If the model doesn't load

If the 3D model fails to load — a slow connection to a bundled asset, an unsupported device, or a
timeout — KDMLink falls back to the same static vehicle image used for cars without a 3D model, so
Home always shows something in that spot.
