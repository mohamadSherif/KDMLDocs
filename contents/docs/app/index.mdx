## The App

Once installed and connected, KDMLink gives you a single place to monitor live data, record
logs, and connect those sessions with RaceChrono. The app has four tabs — **Home**, **Live**,
**Sessions**, and **Settings** — this page is a high-level overview of each; deeper features get
their own page over time.

## Home

The **Home** tab is a customizable dashboard of live widget cards — tire pressure, coolant/oil/
ambient temperature, fuel level, battery voltage, boost pressure, horsepower/torque, and octane
learning status — each sourced from your vehicle's CAN bus. Add, remove, and reorder widgets, and
tap any card for a detail view with recent history. See
[Home Dashboard](/docs/app/dashboard) for the full widget list and how to customize it.

## Live

The **Live** tab is a real-time race dashboard with portrait and full-screen landscape layouts:
gear, a segmented shift light bar, drive mode, and other live signals update continuously while
the N-Link is streaming data. See [Live Race Dashboard](/docs/app/live) for the full field-by-field
breakdown of both layouts.

## Sessions (data logging)

Start and stop recordings from the **Sessions** tab to log a drive for later review. Past
sessions show peak values and personal records, and can be shared as text or a card image. See
[Sessions](/docs/app/sessions) for how recording, personal records, and sharing work.

## RaceChrono integration

Enable RaceChrono in **Settings** to stream KDMLink's CAN-derived vehicle data into your
RaceChrono sessions for lap analysis. See [RaceChrono Integration](/docs/app/racechrono) for setup
steps.
