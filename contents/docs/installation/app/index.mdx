## App Install & Setup

This page covers downloading the KDMLink app, pairing it with your **N-Link**, and getting
through first-run configuration. Make sure the [N-Link hardware](/docs/installation/n-link)
is already installed and powered on before you start.

## First-run configuration

There's no account or sign-in step — KDMLink works fully offline, with no login. On first launch
you'll walk through a short setup flow before reaching the main app:

<Step>
<StepItem title="Welcome">

A branding screen with a **Get Started** button. There's no skip option.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Vehicle selection (required)">

Pick your **Brand → Model → Year → Transmission**. Continue is disabled until a full, valid
vehicle is selected — this step can't be skipped, since your vehicle profile is what drives the
Home dashboard's widgets and units.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Vehicle naming (required)">

Give your vehicle a nickname (prefilled with the model name, so you can just tap through) and
optionally pick a paint color for the [3D vehicle viewer](/docs/app/dashboard/vehicle-viewer).

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Device pairing (optional)">

Tap **Pair Device** to pair your N-Link now, or tap **Skip for now** and pair later. This is also
where the Bluetooth permission prompt appears — see
[Pairing & Connecting](/docs/app/pairing) for the full walkthrough. If you skip this step, you can
pair anytime afterward from **Settings → Connections**.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Setup complete">

A summary of your vehicle and connection status, with a **Continue to Dashboard** button that
finishes setup and takes you to the main app.

</StepItem>
</Step>

Unit preference (imperial/metric) is **not** part of first-run setup — it's a single toggle in
**Settings → Units**, defaulting to imperial (°F, mph). There's no separate PSI/bar-specific
control; it follows the same imperial/metric toggle.

## Updating the app

Updates are delivered entirely through the **App Store** / **Play Store** — there's no in-app
update prompt, and nothing you need to do manually beyond letting your phone update the app as
usual.

<Note type="note" title="App updates vs. N-Link firmware">
Updating the KDMLink app is separate from updating your **N-Link's** firmware. Firmware updates
are handled from **Settings → Firmware Update** and are pushed to the device itself over
Bluetooth — see your N-Link hardware documentation for that process. An app update on its own
does not require re-pairing your N-Link.
</Note>

After an update, you may see a one-time **What's New** walkthrough highlighting recent changes —
this appears automatically and doesn't require any action.

## Troubleshooting
