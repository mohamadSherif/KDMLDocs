## Installation

Getting started with KDMLink takes three steps: download the app, install it, and connect
your device.

<Step>
<StepItem title="Download the app">

Download KDMLink for your platform:

- **Desktop** — Windows and macOS installers (link coming soon)
- **Mobile** — iOS App Store and Google Play (links coming soon)

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Install">

Run the installer (desktop) or install from the app store (mobile) and open KDMLink.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Connect your device">

1. Plug the KDMLink interface device into your vehicle's OBD-II port.
2. Power on the vehicle (ignition on).
3. Connect the device to your phone or computer (USB or Bluetooth, depending on the model).
4. In the app, select your device when prompted to pair.

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Verify the connection">

Once connected, the app should show a live link to your vehicle. You're ready to go.

</StepItem>
</Step>

## Troubleshooting

If the app can't find your device, check that it's properly seated in the OBD-II port, the
vehicle ignition is on, and the connection method (USB or Bluetooth) is enabled. More detailed
troubleshooting will be added here later.
