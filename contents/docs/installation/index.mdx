## Installation

Getting set up with KDMLink has two parts: installing the **N-Link** hardware in your vehicle,
and installing the **KDMLink app** on your phone.

## Download the app

- **iOS** — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kdmlink/id6758346077)
- **Android** — Post your email to @KDMLink on Instagram for early access

## What you'll need

- A supported KDM vehicle
- A KDMLink **N-Link** interface device

Once you've installed the hardware and the app, head to [App Install & Setup](/docs/installation/app)
to pair your phone with the N-Link and verify the connection.
