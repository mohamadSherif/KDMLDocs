## Wire Tap Install

Use this method only when your vehicle requires a wire-tap **N-Link** connection. Current
wire-tap vehicles are listed on [Compatible Vehicles](/docs/compatible-vehicles).

<Step>
<StepItem title="Remove steering wheel lower trim panel">

- Unscrew the screw right below the steering wheel
- Pull the top panel up to release the clips using flathead screwdriver or trim removal tool
- Start the car and turn the steering wheel to the left and right to expose two more screws and remove them
- Pull the bottom panel down to release the clips and remove the panel

![Screws to be removed for accessing the CAN wires](/install/screw_left.png)
![Screws to be removed for accessing the CAN wires](/install/steering_bolt.png)


</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Match the wire colors">

The wire colors on the N-Link CAN harness do not always match the vehicle's CAN harness colors. Use the table below to identify which wire taps into which:

| N-Link CAN harness | Vehicle CAN harness |
| --- | --- |
| <span style={{display:'inline-block',width:'14px',height:'14px',borderRadius:'2px',backgroundColor:'#ffffff',border:'1px solid #888',verticalAlign:'middle',marginRight:'8px'}} />White | <span style={{display:'inline-block',width:'14px',height:'14px',borderRadius:'2px',backgroundColor:'#ffffff',border:'1px solid #888',verticalAlign:'middle',marginRight:'8px'}} />White |
| <span style={{display:'inline-block',width:'14px',height:'14px',borderRadius:'2px',backgroundColor:'#dc2626',verticalAlign:'middle',marginRight:'8px'}} />Red | <span style={{display:'inline-block',width:'14px',height:'14px',borderRadius:'2px',backgroundColor:'#78350f',verticalAlign:'middle',marginRight:'8px'}} />Brown |
| <span style={{display:'inline-block',width:'14px',height:'14px',borderRadius:'2px',backgroundColor:'#000000',verticalAlign:'middle',marginRight:'8px'}} />Black | <span style={{display:'inline-block',width:'14px',height:'14px',borderRadius:'2px',backgroundColor:'#000000',verticalAlign:'middle',marginRight:'8px'}} />Black |

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Tap the required wires">

- Locate and remove the CAN harness on the left side of the steering column.
- Tap the white, brown and black wires on the CAN harness using the supplied posi taps.
- Attach the corresponding wires from the N-Link harness to the posi taps. Confirm the connections are secure.

![Wire tap installation](/install/wire-tap.png)
![Wire tap installation](/install/can_tap.png)
![Tapped wires](/install/tapped_wires.JPG)

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Route and secure the cable">

- Route the cable behind the trim panel around to the fuse box area.
- Plug the power harness into the N-Link.
</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Install the power harness">

- Remove one of the spare fuses in the fuse box.
- Insert the removed fuse into the fuse tap to complete the power connection.
- Plug the power harness fuse tap into the empty slot.
- Find a ground connection point nearby and secure the ground wire with a u-terminal and screw.

![Power harness installed in the fuse box](/install/fuse_box.png)

</StepItem>

<StepItem title="Confirm the N-Link is powered">

Confirm the N-Link powers by checking for the blinking LED light on the module. If the N-Link does not power on, confirm all connections are fully seated and secure.

</StepItem>
</Step>
