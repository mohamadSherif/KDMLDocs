## What's in the box

- N-Link module
- CAN bus harness (for plug-and-play installs) or wire-tap harness (for wire-tap installs)
- Power harness with inline fuse
- KDMLink sticker

## Before you start

Park the vehicle safely, turn it off, and confirm that your phone and vehicle are compatible.
For wire-tap installs, verify the exact wire locations for your vehicle before making any
connections. Required tools:
- Phillips screwdriver
- Pliers or fuse puller (for power harness fuse)
- Zip ties or double-sided tape to secure the N-Link and harnesses

## Compatibility

For vehicle, phone, and install-method requirements, see
[Compatible Vehicles](/docs/compatible-vehicles).
