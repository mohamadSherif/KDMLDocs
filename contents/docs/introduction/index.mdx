## What is KDMLink?

**KDMLink** is a mobile tuning, logging, and diagnostics app for Korean cars (Hyundai, Kia,
and Genesis). It pairs with the **N-Link** interface to read live data from your vehicle,
log sessions, and apply tuning changes — all from your phone.

## Who it's for

KDMLink is built for owners and tuners of Korean performance cars who want to log, diagnose,
and tune their vehicles directly from their phone.

## What you'll need

- A supported Korean vehicle (Hyundai, Kia, or Genesis)
- A KDMLink **N-Link** interface device
- The KDMLink app installed on your iPhone (Android coming soon)

Head to [Installation](/docs/installation) to set everything up.
