## What is KDMLink?

**KDMLink** is a mobile data logging and RaceChrono integration app for Korean cars
(Hyundai, Kia, and Genesis). It pairs with the **N-Link** interface to read live data
from your vehicle, record sessions, and send the data where you need it from your phone.

## Who it's for

KDMLink is built for owners and track drivers of Korean performance cars who want clean,
phone-based logging and RaceChrono workflows.

## What you'll need

- A supported KDM vehicle
- A KDMLink **N-Link** interface device
- The KDMLink app installed on your iPhone (Android coming soon)

Check [Compatible Vehicles](/docs/compatible-vehicles), then head to
[Installation](/docs/installation) to set everything up.
