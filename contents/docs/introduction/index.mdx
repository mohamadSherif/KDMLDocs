## What is KDMLink?

**KDMLink** is a tuning, logging, and diagnostics app for Korean cars (Hyundai, Kia, and
Genesis). It connects to a supported interface device to read live data from your vehicle,
log sessions, and apply tuning changes — available on both desktop and mobile.

## Who it's for

KDMLink is built for owners and tuners of Korean performance cars who want to log, diagnose,
and tune their vehicles directly from a phone or computer.

## What you'll need

- A supported Korean vehicle
- A compatible KDMLink interface device
- The KDMLink app installed on your phone or computer

Head to [Installation](/docs/installation) to set everything up.
